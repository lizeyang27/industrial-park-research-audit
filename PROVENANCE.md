# Provenance

This repository is a privacy-preserving public implementation of a general research-review workflow.

- The public instructions and scripts are newly written for this repository.
- The public industrial-park prior pack newly expresses generalized review questions, distinctions, boundary tests, and evidence requests. It is non-evidentiary and contains no copied mentor comment, meeting passage, employer article, or hidden conclusion.
- Public examples are fictional and generated from scratch for testing.
- No employer document, reviewer comment, private dataset, meeting transcript, production output, or proprietary template is included.
- General review categories may reflect recurring problems seen in research work, but no source wording, distinctive numeric combination, person, organization, or unpublished conclusion is reproduced.
- The public F-style runtime reproduces an organization pattern, not the private experimental F inputs. Authorized private priors, source-support sidecars, and pre-draft manifests remain outside the repository.
- The repository does not claim affiliation with, approval by, or deployment at any employer, client, research institution, or investment organization.

Contributors must confirm that they own or may distribute every contribution and that publication is consistent with confidentiality, employment, and intellectual-property obligations. When in doubt, contribute a newly written synthetic case rather than a redacted real case.
